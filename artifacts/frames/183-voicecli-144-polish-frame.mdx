## Problem

PR #180 merged with a consensus split: 6 blockers + SC-test-4 + 4 cheap advisories landed in the PR; 8 remaining advisories were deferred to post-merge polish. These deferred items span singleton async-safety, store_key validation, test infrastructure refactoring, and single-file hygiene. Leaving them unresolved compounds technical debt in the NATS blobref adapter and test suite.

## Who

- **Primary:** voiceCLI maintainers (code quality + review velocity)
- **Secondary:** Future contributors (cleaner test fixtures, safer runtime)

## Constraints

- Must not break existing CI (ruff, pyright, pytest)
- Must not introduce new review blockers
- Must respect the 3-bundle grouping from the consensus review

## Out of Scope

- Re-opening or re-litigating PR #180 design decisions
- Changes beyond the 8 deferred advisories
- Cross-repo changes (roxabi-blobs upstream)

## Premise Validity

**Success in 6 months:** All 8 items resolved, CI green, `/code-review` on the polish PR produces zero new blockers.

**Failure in 6 months:** Any deferred item still unresolved OR CI/regression introduced by the polish PR.

**Simplest alternative:** Skip polish and leave deferred items as-is.
**Why not simplest:** A2 (singleton async-safety) and A4 (store_key validation) are runtime defense-in-depth; A7-A12 are test-hygiene debt that slows future PR review. Skipping them compounds cost.

## Complexity

**Tier: F-lite** — Clear scope, 3 bundles, no new architecture. Multi-domain (adapters + tests + e2e) but well-defined acceptance criteria.

- ≤10 files across 3 bundles
- No new patterns or dependencies
- Bundles are independent and can be sequenced
